# Nucor baseline footprint — 2025

The latest primary report retrieved is the **2025 Sustainability Report**, linked from Nucor’s live sustainability page through Adobe Publish Online. Its companion SASB index also contains2025 tables. Older direct Nucor PDF URLs still serve2024 tables; these must not silently substitute for the latest baseline. Accessed12September2026.

Nucor is listed in the current S&P500 constituent list (NUE, Materials/Steel). This was verified against a current secondary list, not a licensed index-provider snapshot. [Constituent list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_S%26P_500_companies).

## Boundary-critical findings

- Company total19.2MtCO2e and steel-mill17.7MtCO2e are overlapping inventories, not additive. Steel mill Scope3 includes upstream material emissions including internal supply; own DRI reductions may migrate between scopes.
- The published0.77tCO2e/t steel and1.051tCO2e/t hot-rolled steel are different boundaries. The former includes all melting mills in intensity while the17.7Mt inventory excludes Lexington/Kingman. A 23Mt cast denominator is not a verified hot-rolled denominator.
- EAF share is already100%; there is no legitimate companywide blast-furnace-to-EAF switching opportunity.
- Exact market/location Scope2 breakdown was not retrieved. Reported totals must remain method-unspecified.

## Candidate intervention inputs

- **Additional low-carbon electricity plus EAF efficiency:** Enterprise18.398TWh; mills residual16.996TWh estimated. Grid/contractual accounting unresolved. Separate efficiency load reduction then supply decarbonization; existing PPAs already reflected. Firming requirements and hourly/location impact.
- **Reheat efficiency/hot charging and carbon-injection optimization:** Mills58.727PJfuel,71%NG and24%coal; aggregate screening only. No site process split or installed technology inventory. Preserve steel grade/quality/output; company2030 plan overlaps.
- **Cleaner virgin iron and enhanced scrap sorting:** 3.37Mt own DRI delivered; >2m gross tons pigiron received. Copper/tin limits; availability and embodied factors; never count own-DRI reduction in companyScope1 plus millScope3 twice.
- **Water reuse and waste recovery:** 41.3m m3withdrawal;17.3m discharge; enterprise69%reuse. Plant/basin and contaminant quality needed; recirculation rates cannot be treated as remaining freshwater abatement shares.

## Missing data and explicit uncertainty

- 2025 exact Scope2 market- and location-based numbers and supplier/residual emission factors, contractual-instrument reconciliation.
- 2025 SCS verification statements, assurance level/materiality and exact covered facility list. PDF here text is not an active link in downloaded PDF.
- Complete15-category Scope3 inventory and Category1 decomposition between external pig iron, own DRI, purchased DRI, alloys, electrodes, scrap and transport; corporate intercompany eliminations.
- Matching GSCC hot-rolled steel production denominator and asset/product coverage; do not multiply hot-rolled kg/t by cast23Mt.
- Energy by facility/process, existing preheaters/reheat furnace status, MWh matched to incremental carbon-free contracts, downtime and quality limits.
- Feedstock actually charged by mill/grade; scrap copper/tin quality and yields; DRI/pig iron quantities produced vs supplied/received and their life-cycle factors.
- Water measured consumption/storage change, facility/basin withdrawal/discharge/consumption and effluent quality; water-stress percentages are not volume shares.
- Waste exact short/metric ton definition; slag/dust product-versus-waste classifications, hazardous endpoints and slight rounded-total mismatch.
