## Source and measurement audit

The company reports 142,000 tonnes methane in 2025, 96% upstream, with the rounded chart assigning 5% to tanks/storage: approximately 6,800 tonnes methane. The chart totals 101%; do not renormalize. Existing tank VRU retrofits and closed-vessel new builds mean this is not an untreated inventory. More than 10,000 pneumatics have been eliminated since 2023, including over 6,000 Pioneer devices in 2025. More than 400 heritage Permian sites have continuous monitoring; key-site expansion is planned for Permian by end-2026 and global upstream by 2028. Updated emission factors increased estimated fugitives in 2025; measured physical change cannot be inferred directly. [Company methane chapter](https://corporate.exxonmobil.com/Publications/Advancing-Climate-Solutions/driving-reductions-in-methane-emissions).

OGMP Pathway recognition means an implementation path, not proof every site is already reconciled to measurements. Level 5 reconciles source inventories with site measurements. No defensible company-wide measured/reporting uplift was found. [OGMP FAQ](https://www.ogmpartnership.org/frequently-asked-questions).

The operated 2025 baseline is 90 million tonnes Scope 1, or 97 million including market-based Scope 2. The data page says 2025 independent assurance is underway; 2024 received limited assurance. Do not label 2025 fully independently assured. [Metrics table](https://corporate.exxonmobil.com/publications/metrics-and-data).

## What current technology establishes

EPA supports a 95% annual eligible-source factor, partly reflecting maintenance availability. It requires reliable power, suitable flows and a usable gas outlet. Existing-control failures require measured repair counterfactuals, not automatic application of 95% to the remaining residual. EPA also supports VOC/HAP co-benefits, without company pollutant quantities. [EPA guidance](https://www.epa.gov/natural-gas-star-program/vapor-recovery-units).

Using fossil methane GWP100 29.8 and complete combustion CO2 of 2.75 tonnes per tonne methane gives 27.05 tonnes CO2e per tonne captured, before power and other lifecycle emissions. GWP20 is 82.5; choosing that horizon changes the climate metric, not the physical tonnes. [IPCC Table 7.15](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_Chapter07.pdf).

Zero remains possible. These are arbitrary sensitivities, not probabilities or bounds on true physical emissions. The 100% row is a ceiling conditional on the reported source pool and eligibility. Because downstream combustion enters the numerator, the Scope 1 ratio is scale context, not an inventory reduction percentage.

## Power and investment

For a metered project, net annual climate benefit = captured methane × 27.05 − electricity kWh × grid kgCO2e/kWh ÷ 1,000 − other incremental lifecycle emissions. At an **assumed** 1,000 kWh per captured tonne and **assumed** 0.4 kgCO2e/kWh, net benefit is 26.65 tonnes CO2e per tonne, 1.5% below the pre-power figure. At 5,000 kWh and 0.8 kg, it is 23.05, 14.8% below. These are transparent diagnostic sensitivities, not Exxon loads or regional grid measurements. Compressor horsepower cannot establish fleet electricity without unit count, loading, duty cycle and motor efficiency.

EPA's archived equipment study gives a 100-Mcf/day design, half-capacity average feed, $55,524 installed cost and $10,103 annual O&M. At 95% recovery, annual mixed-vapor recovery is 17,337.5 Mcf. Simple payback calculated independently is 1.325 years at $3/Mcf, 0.725 at $5, and 0.499 at $7; source discounted-payback entries are different metrics. The five-year undiscounted principal-plus-O&M threshold is $1.223/Mcf before omitted costs. This is historical nominal engineering evidence archived by 2021; exact underlying cost dollar year is unverified. Pipeline connection costs remain site specific, and electricity can already be included in O&M. [EPA archived report, Exhibits 6–7](https://19january2021snapshot.epa.gov/sites/static/files/2016-06/documents/ll_final_vap.pdf).

The existing pilot used an earlier EPA version's $41,125 installed and $7,200 O&M. Keep that version label or replace it with the separate archived example; do not blend costs or call either a current quote. [Earlier EPA report, Exhibits 5–6](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P1004FDH.TXT).

Repayment comes from incremental gas receipts, not avoided social damage. Current Exxon capex, funding additionality and payback remain unknown. Meter methane fraction separately from mixed gas volumes; obtain ownership/offtake, power and connection quotes, operating schedules and maintenance provisions. Reconcile existing company spending and required actions before attributing anything to a fund. Count a leak repaired through a VRU only once.
