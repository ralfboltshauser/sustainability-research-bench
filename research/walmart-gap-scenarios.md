# Walmart: quantified conditional refrigeration opportunity

**Qualified pitch:** Walmart reports **4.08 million tonnes CO2e per year from onsite refrigerants**. A real supermarket comparison shows that commercially available CO2 cooling can nearly eliminate direct refrigerant climate impact. **If systems responsible for half of that Walmart source were eligible and converted by 2031 with comparable performance, gross direct avoidance would be about 2.04 million tonnes/year, or 14.2% of operational emissions.** Energy, lifecycle effects, feasible adoption and investor additionality still require validation.

This is a conditional engineering opportunity—not a finding that half the emissions are proven unnecessary, a 2031 forecast, or a monetary damage estimate.

## Baseline climate pressure

Walmart FY2026 onsite refrigerants: **4.08 MtCO2e/year**, versus **14.40 MtCO2e/year** Scope 1 plus market-based Scope 2. The source is **28.33%** of the rounded operational inventory. These quantify climate pressure, not dollars of loss. [Walmart report, p.31](https://corporate.walmart.com/content/dam/corporate/documents/esgreport/2026/FY2026-Walmart-ESG-Report.pdf)

## Independent original deployment evidence

The DOE/Navigant Hannaford case compares CO2 refrigeration at Turner, Maine with HFC-407A equipment at Bradford, Vermont. Table V.2 reports equal leaked mass of **200 lb** at both stores, but **191 versus 0.1 tonnes CO2e** direct emissions: approximately **99.95% less direct refrigerant climate impact**. Layout and refrigeration loads were nearly identical. This is measured paired-store evidence with calculated climate equivalents, not a randomized trial. The table period is September 2013–July 2014, although surrounding prose describes a year. [DOE study, printed pp.19–20, Table V.2](https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/2015/02/f19/Hannaford%20Study%20Report%201-22-2015_CLEAN.pdf)

The same table reports whole-store emissions of 1,179 versus 1,003 tonnes, approximately 15% lower, not 99.95% lower: electricity was almost unchanged and propane use increased. Thus the near-total direct reduction must never be used as whole-store/lifecycle efficacy. The figures are rounded.

Commercial maturity is separately supported by ORNL reporting more than 130 North American supermarket installations of the transcritical system within two years; this is deployment evidence, not a Walmart adoption forecast. [ORNL, July 2025](https://www.ornl.gov/success-story/refrigerating-carbon-dioxide-boosts-performance?check_logged_in=1)

## Fixed-activity conditional scenarios

Let **q** be the fraction of baseline *refrigerant emissions*, not stores, that is both eligible and replaced by 2031. Let **r=1−0.1/191≈99.95%** be the transferred direct process effect. Then **gross annual direct avoidance=4.08×q×r MtCO2e**. Keep cooling service, food volumes and temperatures unchanged.

| Explicit conditional scenario—not verified adoption | Gross direct avoided MtCO2e/year | Share of onsite refrigerants avoided | Share of operational inventory avoided |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| If q=25% and Hannaford-like direct efficacy applies | 1.0195 | 24.99% | 7.08% |
| If q=50% and Hannaford-like direct efficacy applies | 2.0389 | 49.97% | 14.16% |
| If q=100% and Hannaford-like direct efficacy applies | 4.0779 | 99.95% | 28.32% |

**Elimination ceiling, separately:** removing all onsite refrigerant emissions gives 4.08 MtCO2e/year, or 28.33% of operational emissions. This is a source-accounting ceiling, not demonstrated 2031 feasibility. Actual CO2 systems still have small direct emissions.

## Conditions and penalties

- **Gas mix:** the case uses HFC-407A, not Walmart’s unknown blend. General direct efficacy is 1−(new leaked kg/old leaked kg)×(new GWP/old GWP). Match gas-specific inventory and GWP versions; do not treat a table ratio as universal.
- **Energy and lifecycle:** net avoidance=gross direct avoidance−additional electricity emissions−additional fuel emissions−incremental lifecycle emissions. Negative penalties are savings. Current site electricity/heating and embodied-equipment inputs are unknown, so net results remain null. At q=50%, combined additional annual impacts exceeding about 2.039 MtCO2e would erase the modeled direct benefit. This threshold is arithmetic, not a prediction.
- **Adoption:** q=25%, 50%, 100% are explicit sensitivity assumptions. None is claimed feasible. Current low-GWP deployments are already in the baseline; do not count them again. A practical schedule needs system age, engineering fit, climate, labour capacity, suppliers and approved replacements.
- **Attribution and money:** deduct upgrades that would happen without the proposed fund. Avoided climate impact is not cash revenue; principal repayment needs operating savings or enforceable project payments.
- **Timing:** outputs are annual run-rates at fixed activity after conversion, not cumulative five-year reductions. No installation ramp has been assumed.

The most useful conclusion is a material, evidence-grounded **conditional** opportunity: eligible refrigeration could contribute meaningfully to operational reductions with existing technology. The public data do not yet identify its feasible 2031 size or a verified “unnecessary harm” total.
