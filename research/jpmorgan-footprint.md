## Financing and current policy
[Report](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmorganchase/documents/about/jpmc-sustainability-report-2024.pdf), pp 9–10,18,22–23:2024 energy-supply financing was $36.4bn low-carbon and $32.3bn high-carbon, ratio1.13. Green-eligible financing/facilitation was $68bn in 2024,$309bn cumulative 2021–2024, against $1tn by 2030. These are transaction-accounting measures, not verified abatement or incremental capital. Operational deadlines/percentage targets have been replaced by a decision framework. Business travel emissions are no longer disclosed.

## Inputs still needed for a fixed-service 2031 intervention estimate
- Borrower identities, LEIs, facility ownership, geolocation, baseline physical emissions and output by asset; borrower reporting vintage and uncertainty.
- Complete exposure and emissions inventory across asset classes; explicit portfolio coverage denominators and treatment of unreported clients/scopes.
- Intervention-specific retrofit eligibility, capex/opex, energy prices, utilization, useful life, deployment schedule by 2031, and service/output held constant.
- Matched financing counterfactual: would asset owner implement without JPMorgan, alternative lenders, financing terms, covenants and completion evidence.
- Absolute changes separated into borrower physical performance, production/output, portfolio entry/exit, exposure weights, market valuations, methodology and proxy changes.
- Power hourly/location matching and procurement additionality; no inference of zero grid emissions from EAC-backed zero market-based electricity.
- Methane direct measurements versus estimates; fossil CH4 GWP basis and gas-specific data for interventions.
- Supplier Scope3 and business-travel completeness; no all-company harm total.
- Water, land, biodiversity, local air pollutants, resource extraction and social impacts need separate location-specific inventories.

## Duplicate emissions control
Maintain one physical-emissions ledger keyed by borrower asset, gas and year, with separate bank financing-allocation records. Match borrower identities before combining this pilot with industrial-company inventories. Fuel combustion can recur in oil/gas end use, power, transport, customer Scope2/3 and lender portfolios. Portfolio sales, financing changes and valuation shifts do not establish atmospheric reductions. No portfolio total is calculated.

Operational sums and financing ratio pass arithmetic checks. Portfolio percentages retain reported precision. Originals and the parsed holdings workbook are saved in sources/jpmorgan.
