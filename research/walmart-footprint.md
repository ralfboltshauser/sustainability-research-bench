## Interpretation and checks

Scope1 sources sum8.16MMT; Scope2 market components sum6.24MMT; total14.4MMT. Location-based alternative total is17.82MMT, not an additional footprint. Percentages in the source are rounded. Onsite refrigerants account for about half of Scope1.

The Scope3 estimate635MMT is a broad model covering four principal categories and selected markets; lifetime use of products sold is included. It is not purely annual emissions physically occurring during the fiscal year. Supplier Project Gigaton numbers are a separate project-accounting ledger, never a subtraction from the inventory.

Food landfill/incineration1420million lb converts exactly to644101.1654tonnes using0.45359237kg/lb. All six pathways sum3493.2million lb, but this is not automatically the FLW definition of waste: donation/feed are excluded, compost/digestion included. A treatment shift can improve diversion while worsening FLW intensity. The644101tonnes is not all preventable food.

619 U.S. facilities includes partial conversion. Do not divide by store count to infer a global completed conversion fraction. Electricity renewable53.3% includes grids; contracts35.0% have a different boundary. Clean generation capacity in MW is not MWh consumption or annual avoided emissions.

## Inputs for adoption by2031 at fixed service volume

### Refrigerant leakage and low-GWP replacement

At fixed refrigeration service, allocate4.08MMT across existing equipment cohorts; multiply cohort emissions by adoption and residual leakage/GWP improvement. Apply leak management and replacement sequentially on shared cohorts.

Control: direct. Missing: charge mass and refrigerant species/GWP, actual leak rate, U.S./global split, system age, replacement schedule and cost, incremental electricity, technician capacity.

### Electricity efficiency and additional clean supply

MWh saved times appropriate marginal/attributional emissions factors; then procurement on residual load. Maintain floor area, opening hours, refrigeration service and merchandise throughput. Track location and market accounting separately.

Control: direct demand and procurement; grid largely external. Missing: MWh by country/end-use/hour, residual equipment stock, project capex, tariffs, grid factors, procurement terms.

### Fleet efficiency/electrification and stationary heat electrification

Fuel savings at fixed delivered tonne-km; heat pumps at fixed useful heat. Net Scope1 decrease against added Scope2 and upstream fuel/electricity effects.

Control: direct, with technology/infrastructure dependencies. Missing: fuel species/volumes, transport-refrigerant split, tonnes/miles/hours, heat load/COP, electricity factors, asset replacement capacity.

### Food prevention, donation and disposal treatment

CY2025 landfill/incineration644101t is bounded physical pool, not entirely preventable. Split edible/avoidable and landfill/incineration, food categories and sites; hold edible food service fixed, reduce procurement for prevented waste. Account upstream avoided production only where displaced, and residual treatment separately.

Control: direct inventory and handling; suppliers and disposal contractors shared. Missing: avoidable fractions, food-category composition, sold tonnes, landfill methane capture, disposal split, program costs and displacement evidence.

### Supplier product/process changes

Build product-specific life-cycle inventory and allocated Walmart purchasing quantities. Do not apply arbitrary reduction rate to635MMT or claim Gigaton reductions as inventory abatement.

Control: supplier influence/shared, customer use largely indirect. Missing: category-resolved Scope3, product quantities, supplier-specific factors, attribution, physical product lifetime and use assumptions.
