S&P500 membership verified through [State Street index holdings](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/individual/etfs/state-street-energy-select-sector-spdr-etf-xle), dated September10,2026: its Energy Select Sector universe is drawn from S&P500 and lists Exxonmobil Holdings Corporation.

## Assurance and independent records

[ERM assurance](https://corporate.exxonmobil.com/-/media/global/files/advancing-climate-solutions/2026/erm-cvs-limited-assurance-report-for-exxonmobil.pdf) pp1–3 is limited assurance of selected **2024 operated** GHG information. Its scope does not establish assurance for2025, equity emissions, Scope3, water or other pollutants. The current company data page says2025 assurance is underway. Reported inventories combine measurements and estimates; confidence in transcription is higher than confidence in complete physical quantification.

The [EPA Joliet docket](https://yosemite.epa.gov/OA/RHC/EPAAdmin.nsf/All%2BDockets%2Bby%2BCase%2BNumber/CWA-05-2026-0010) lists a closed Clean Water Act case with payment received inJuly2026. This validates a local enforcement record, not an additional global emissions estimate. No penalty is converted into environmental damage.

## Fixed2025 activity, interventions adopted by2031

- **Methane equipment/source controls**: 142ktCH4;96% upstream; source shares. Calculation: 142000*0.96; source mass*eligible fraction*incremental2031 adoption*verified effectiveness. Missing: asset source-level inventory; counterfactual commitments; measured source rates; feasible replacement/repair cycles; fuel recovered destination. Overlap: flares, engine electrification and methane capture count once; recovered gas combustion changes CO2.
- **Flaring reduction**: 150MMscfd all;4MMscfd routine upstream;5MtCO2e flaring. Calculation: 4*365/1000; calculate species mass and combustion outcomes separately. Missing: gas composition; heating value; destruction efficiency; capture routes/capacity; safety/nonroutine shares. Overlap: methane flaring pool and captured gas combustion; do not claim all5Mt addressable.
- **Electrification/clean electricity**: 1.4billionGJ energy;9Mt location and8Mt market Scope2; sector totals. Calculation: displaced fuel emissions minus incremental grid emissions and lifecycle change at same delivered work. Missing: electricity MWh, fuel/process-temperature inventory, motor/heat efficiencies, hourly marginal grid factors, grid capacity and completion dates. Overlap: engine methane slip and energy efficiency; Scope2 accounting certificates not proof of physical avoidance.
- **Water/pollution prevention**: water and species-level global totals. Calculation: site intervention efficacy applied to eligible pathway inventory; damages assessed separately. Missing: basin-season stress, receiving water quality, pollutant speciation, population exposure, facility inventory.

Do not treat equipment or fuel sales growth as environmental improvement at fixed service. Methane recovery can move carbon from methane to CO2; calculate both. Improved operational intensity does not remove product-combustion emissions. A commitment is neither completed abatement nor automatically additional to a baseline.
