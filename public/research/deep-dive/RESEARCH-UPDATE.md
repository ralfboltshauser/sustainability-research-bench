# Expanded preventable-impact research

The pilot now covers eight companies. Its purpose is to identify specific environmental burdens, examine available alternatives, and expose the evidence needed to turn an engineering comparison into a credible investment opportunity. It does not establish a comparable whole-company preventable-harm ranking.

The expansion adds UPS, Delta Air Lines and Duke Energy, and deepens the Walmart, ExxonMobil and Microsoft dossiers. The application provides an evidence ledger with original source links, dates, units, locations within documents, and explicit distinctions between inventory observations, modeled comparisons, assumptions, achieved progress and projections. Source count is a coverage measure, not a statistical confidence score.

UPS’s main GRI inventory differs from its segment-total table by 0.001 million tonnes; the discrepancy remains visible. Duke’s direct figure is a sum of nine rounded components, not a separately reported exact total. Delta’s operational-control inventory excludes Monroe refinery. These boundaries prevent treating the direct emissions table as an all-environmental-performance comparison. [UPS inventory](https://about.ups.com/content/dam/upsstories/images/our-impact/reporting/2025-ups-gri.pdf), [Delta inventory](https://esghub.delta.com/content/esg/en/2025/ghg-emissions-data.html), [Duke metrics](https://s201.q4cdn.com/583395453/files/doc_downloads/2026/2025-Sustainability-Metrics-Center.pdf).

For UPS, the original NREL evaluation reports 1,414.93 diesel versus 759.06 electric grams CO₂e per mile. The 46.35% reduction is specific to the historical operating and local-electricity assumptions. Using the study’s national electricity case lowers the relative reduction to 32.3%. Neither is an observed UPS fleet result. [NREL evaluation, pp. 20–21](https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy17osti/66382.pdf).

Duke’s process comparison starts with otherwise-vented gas in eligible maintenance events. EPA documents recovery fractions of 50–90%. The calculation uses fossil methane GWP100 of 29.8 and subtracts 2.75 tonnes CO₂ per tonne of recovered methane burned. It takes no displacement credit and does not assume that all reported gas-distribution methane is maintenance venting. [EPA pump-down guidance](https://19january2017snapshot.epa.gov/sites/production/files/2016-06/documents/ll_pipeline.pdf), [IPCC AR6 factors](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGIII_Annex-II.pdf).

Delta’s gate benchmark concerns comparable electrical and thermal-comfort service. The original author’s methods establish that its 63–97% environmental reduction compares a partly electrified baseline (70% electric ground power with diesel cooling, 30% APU) with fully electric power and cooling. Manufacturing is included; end-of-life is excluded. The study’s 1–2-year economic payback uses a different, 100%-APU baseline. These are not interchangeable counterfactuals. [Original-author methods, Tables 7–8 and Figure 10](https://escholarship.org/content/qt08w701t9/qt08w701t9.pdf). Grid mix, aircraft types, operating durations and actual equipment connection determine performance. The company already reports operational fuel savings and electrified infrastructure; those are not automatically new funding opportunities. [Study institutional record](https://its.berkeley.edu/publications/reduce-aviation%E2%80%99s-greenhouse-gas-emissions-through-immediately-feasible-and-affordable), [Delta progress](https://esghub.delta.com/content/esg/en/2025/advancing-progress.html).

## Corrections and deeper findings

**Walmart.** The 4.08-million-tonne refrigerant baseline remains supported. The company reports 619 U.S. facilities partly or fully using ultra-low-GWP systems, but neither a complete-conversion count nor an emissions-weighted denominator. An approximately 80% footprint change at selected projects refers to installed legacy refrigerants, not annual leakage. Existing capital plans fund replacements. Consequently the 25%, 50% and 100% cases remain explicit sensitivities; zero additional funding impact is also possible. [Walmart report, p. 31](https://corporate.walmart.com/content/dam/corporate/documents/esgreport/2026/FY2026-Walmart-ESG-Report.pdf).

The old paired-store study’s direct refrigerant numbers are accurately copied. Its whole-store propane row, however, implies an emission factor inconsistent with the printed energy unit and EPA combustion factors. This is an unresolved unit/conversion discrepancy, not proof of its cause. Diagnostic recalculation is not a validated replacement estimate. The application does not use the source’s approximately 15% whole-store difference as a net Walmart reduction. [Detailed Walmart audit](walmart-deep.md), [original study](https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/2015/02/f19/Hannaford%20Study%20Report%201-22-2015_CLEAN.pdf).

**ExxonMobil.** The approximate 6,800-tonne methane tank-source pool is not an untreated eligible inventory. Reported inventories combine estimation and observation; emission-factor changes affect trends. Gold Standard Pathway recognition and monitoring coverage must not be relabeled as complete measured verification. The company describes assurance of its 2025 inventory as underway. Historical EPA recovery-unit economics are shown separately from current project economics. [Methane disclosure](https://corporate.exxonmobil.com/Publications/Advancing-Climate-Solutions/driving-reductions-in-methane-emissions), [inventory](https://corporate.exxonmobil.com/publications/metrics-and-data), [detailed audit](exxon-deep.md).

**Microsoft.** The 15% matched lifecycle climate comparison remains a process benchmark. Phoenix’s 23% FY25 WUE improvement is achieved intensity progress, not an absolute saved-water volume. The greater-than-125,000 m³-per-site design figure concerns projected withdrawal avoidance, not metered consumption savings. Fairwater’s reported April 2026 commissioning strengthens deployment evidence, but does not reveal the remaining eligible fleet. Water-efficient cooling can increase power demand. [Water-efficiency update](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/06/24/inside-microsofts-two-decade-push-to-cut-water-intensity-while-scaling-for-growth/), [design and tradeoff](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-cloud/blog/2024/12/09/sustainable-by-design-next-generation-datacenters-consume-zero-water-for-cooling/), [commissioning statement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q3), [detailed audit](microsoft-deep.md).

## What the investment thesis can claim

The evidence identifies promising project categories: refrigerant leak prevention and equipment replacement, suitable methane recovery, eligible delivery-route electrification, gate power and cooling, and specific industrial and data-center controls. It does not yet supply an underwritten billion-dollar allocation.

A project needs a named asset, a service-matched baseline, net physical reductions, actual deployment capacity, capital and operating costs, and a repayment contract. Existing or committed improvements belong in the counterfactual. Financing must cause extra or earlier improvements to claim additional impact. Reported footprint reductions caused only by accounting changes or selling financial exposures are not prevented physical damage.

Monetization uses $255.12 per tonne CO₂e in 2024 dollars from Global Value Factors Database V4. It is a fixed-factor screening model for future societal climate damage associated with emissions. GWP equivalence is not exact gas-specific damage equivalence. These amounts are not observed company costs, all environmental harm, or cash available to repay capital. [Valuation workbook](https://capitalscoalition.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Global-Value-Factors-Database-V4-2026-External.xlsx).

Water consumption, pollutant masses and local exposure remain separate measures. Without location-specific pathways, populations and ecological context, those pressures cannot be translated responsibly into a common monetary damage ranking.
